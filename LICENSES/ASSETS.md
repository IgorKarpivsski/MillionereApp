# Asset and content provenance

Every visual, audio, font and text asset shipped in the app is listed here with
its source and license. Nothing goes into `apps/mobile/assets` without a row.

## Rules (from the architecture doc, section 11)

- No club, league, competition or national-team logos, crests or kits.
- No player photographs, likenesses or official trading-card designs.
- No broadcast footage, stadium recordings, chants or official music.
- No artwork, sounds or wording from any TV quiz show.
- Real player names on collectibles only after a license is in place
  (`collectibles.license_status`). Trivia questions may name real people and
  events as facts, with a source stored per question.
- "World Cup", "Champions League", "Ballon d'Or" and similar names may appear
  in question text; they are not used as names of albums, packs, categories
  or the app itself.
